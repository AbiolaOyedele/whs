    body: 'A storefront that opens on a full-width photo of each collection, with every piece priced in naira.     body: 'At a market she picks a piece by its code and the shop makes a QR code the customer pays through on their own phone. Bags left unpaid for twenty minutes are listed so she can follow up. Visitors are counted on the shop itself, with no ad trackers. QR codes printed on delivery bags report every scan and can be pointed somewhere new after printing. Takings, costs and discount codes live in the same place.'
